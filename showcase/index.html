Why JeevanRakshak?

Mine emergencies can simultaneously remove visibility, positioning and communication. R01 is designed to enter first, build situational awareness and keep critical perception and navigation onboard even when the surface link is interrupted.

Autonomous reconnaissance

LiDAR, RGB-D and onboard SLAM provide measured tunnel geometry and rover localization without GPS.

Multi-sensor victim evidence

Person detection is fused with D435 depth ranging and AMG8833 thermal evidence for possible-victim reporting.

Resilient communications

A normal relay path carries broadband mission data, while JR_BYPASS is designed to restore connectivity around a failed wired segment.

Base Station Command Center

The command center is the explanation and supervision layer. It visualizes what R01 publishes rather than replacing onboard autonomy.

Perception

RGB/night view, depth, thermal grid, person confidence, range and victim-fusion state.

Digital twin

Mine map, rover pose, planned/travelled route, semantic markers and navigation state.

Network recovery

Relay topology, failed segment, bypass state, deployed-node visualization and mission timeline.

Prototype status

This is a research prototype. The rover/base stack has been developed as separable, testable subsystems so mapping, navigation, perception, telemetry and communication recovery can be validated independently before final integrated operation.
